      Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)

      Pain Reliever & Fever Reducer
      Brand Examples: Dolo 650, Crocin, Calpol
      What It Is Used For

      Relieves mild to moderate headaches, fever, and muscle pain.

      How & When to Take

      Take with water. Can be taken with or without food. Maximum 4,000 mg in 24 hours.

      Important Precautions

      Avoid alcohol. Overdose can cause liver damage. Consult doctor if fever lasts over 3 days.

      Possible Side Effects

      Rare when taken as directed: nausea, mild skin itch.
